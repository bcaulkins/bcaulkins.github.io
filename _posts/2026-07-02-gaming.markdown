I've been going through a bit of a rough patch with gaming lately. I mainly play this survival RPG/MMO-lite game called Dune Awakening. I have around 80 hours invested and it's fun, but it's not always what I'm in the mood for.

Then I have Blue Prince which I started not too long ago. It's this unique rogue like game where you try to find your way through this mansion and get this inheritance from your relative. I love the concept of it, I do plan on playing more of it, but it's not the pace I'm looking for.

Next up I have tried Expedition 33. It's a fabulous game. Stunning, beautiful, has a top notch sound track. But like Dune, it's not what I'm in the mood for.

Just today I re-installed a game I had purchased a while back, Horizon Zero Dawn. It's 10 years old, but my god is it exactly what I needed. It has the grind of an MMO with resources, but the single player nature is tight, combat is hectic yet rewarding. I came downstairs to play more and I just had to write about it, tell someone. Tell myself in this blog.

If you haven't played HZD, do yourself a favor and go get it. It's phenomenal and it renewed my joy in gaming.
